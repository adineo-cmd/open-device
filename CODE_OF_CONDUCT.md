# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

We are a research project. We disagree about instruction sets, container
layouts and prior art — not about people. Be precise, be generous, and assume
the other person is trying to help.

## Expected behaviour

- Critique claims with evidence, not credentials or volume.
- Credit prior work, including work that contradicts your own.
- Record negative results and corrections honestly.
- Respect the scope in `SCOPE.md` and the no-blobs rule in `CONTRIBUTING.md`.
- Use issue comments for coordination; do not claim an issue already claimed by
  someone else (`AGENTS.md`, Rule 2).

## Unacceptable behaviour

Harassment, personal attacks, discrimination, doxxing, deliberate publication
of device-private data, and knowingly committing proprietary payloads. Prior-art
claims made without searches, and findings presented as unique when the author
knew of prior art, are also unacceptable research misconduct here.

## Scope

This applies to all repository spaces: issues, pull requests, discussions,
commit messages and any project chat.

## Enforcement

Report incidents privately to the repository owner via GitHub. Reports are
handled confidentially. Consequences range from a private warning to a ban,
proportional to the incident. The owner may remove comments, commits or PRs
that violate this document or `SCOPE.md`.
